Good Neighbor Agents - Some of the most valuable work happens between people, and it's usually run by volunteers and tiny teams stretched thin. This track is about local life: matching volunteers to the shifts a food bank actually needs, helping a small nonprofit answer questions and coordinate events without a full-time coordinator, keeping a community group's members in the loop, or turning a library's or school's scattered info into something anyone can just ask. Good Neighbor Agents help a group of people and lightens the load on those already holding that group together. If your agent makes a community more organized, more connected, or more resilient, this is its home.

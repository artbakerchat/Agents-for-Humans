## Overview
*Nechako: It Will Be a Big River Again* is a crucial documentary that follows two Indigenous Nations fighting for our collective future. 

When the Kenney Dam was built in the 1950s, the Nechako River was forever changed. This film explores the ongoing environmental impact, the history of the dam, and the active efforts of local First Nations to restore the river's flow and ecosystem for future generations.

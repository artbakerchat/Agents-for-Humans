## Event Details
* **Date:** Saturday, September 26, 2026
* **Time:** 2:00 p.m. – 4:30 p.m.
* **Venue:** Welsh Hall, West Vancouver Memorial Library
* **Admission:** Free (Registration Required)
* **Special Guest:** Includes a post-screening talk back with Geoffrey Erickson, Director of the Arthur Erickson Foundation and nephew of Arthur Erickson.

## Overview
*Arthur Erickson: Beauty Between the Lines* offers an intimate look into the life, vision, and legacy of Arthur Erickson, one of Canada's most celebrated modern architects. 

The documentary examines his iconic structures, his revolutionary design philosophy blending architecture with landscapes, and the dynamic personal and professional life behind his world-renowned creations.

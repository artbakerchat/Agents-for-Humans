# Movie Matinée: Aberdeen

## Event Details
* **Date:** Wednesday, September 30, 2026 (National Day for Truth and Reconciliation)
* **Time:** 2:00 p.m. – 3:30 p.m.
* **Venue:** Welsh Hall, West Vancouver Memorial Library
* **Admission:** Free (Registration Required)

## Overview
Screened on the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation, *Aberdeen* is a raw, authentic feature drama interwoven with pockets of humor. 

The film centers on Aberdeen, an Indigenous woman navigating urban life, family bonds, structural challenges, and personal resilience. It delivers an unforgettable character study filled with nuance, heart, and realistic storytelling.

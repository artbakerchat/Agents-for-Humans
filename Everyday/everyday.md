Everyday Agents - Everyday life is full of small, repetitive tasks that nobody enjoys and everybody forgets like bills creeping up on their due dates, warranties and returns with expiring windows or a family calendar that lives in four different heads. Everyday Agents take that load off. The strongest projects here won’t hand you another app to babysit, they’ll work in the background, make the safe calls on their own and surface only when a human actually needs to weigh in. Think of an agent that watches for bills and flags or schedules them before they're late, one that keeps a household's schedules in sync across everyone, or one that tracks what you bought and quietly handles the return before the window closes. If it makes an ordinary day smoother without adding a single chore, it belongs here.
